# Fire Engine Ambulance Police Car: Specs, Pricing and Import Guide

> 消防车 救护车 警用 汽车

Applications for the fire engine ambulance police car span municipal services, construction sites, industrial facilities and agricultural operations. In African markets, government and NGO procurement drives significant demand, while in Southeast Asia the private construction sector is the primary buyer. Latin American buyers increasingly source directly from Chinese factories to bypass import markup layers.

The fire engine ambulance police car is a purpose-built 消防应急 vehicle designed for commercial and industrial applications. Export models from Chinese manufacturers typically feature proven chassis options including Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW, ISUZU and MAN, with factory-direct FOB pricing that runs 30-40% below local dealer prices in most destination markets.

For buyers importing a fire engine ambulance police car from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

Proper maintenance of a fire engine ambulance police car centers on the PTO engagement system, hydraulic oil quality, and body mounting integrity. Most Chinese export models are designed for simple field maintenance with standard tools. A complete spare parts kit (filters, seals, hydraulic hoses) should be ordered with the initial purchase to ensure availability in remote operating locations.

Expect to pay $28,000-85,000 FOB for a properly specced unit.
