## Field Notes

In practical field use, a stronger manufacturer brand usually means better boom alignment, cleaner hydraulic routing, and more stable platform operation after months of vibration. Buyers should ask for real photos of the subframe, winch drum, tilt system, and rear support structure. A cheap build may look acceptable at delivery, but weak coating, poor hose protection, or low-grade cylinders will increase downtime and repair costs.

For overseas importers, the safest strategy is simple: choose a proven chassis, inspect the manufacturer’s recovery body closely, and judge the deal by operating cost, not just sticker price.
