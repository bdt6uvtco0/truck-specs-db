## Field Notes

The most common variants are the synchronous chip sealer, the independently controlled chip spreader, and the integrated asphalt chip sealer truck with optional spray bar adjustments. In practice, the synchronous type is popular because it saves time and reduces mismatch between binder and stone application. However, buyers who work on mixed road conditions sometimes prefer a more flexible model with separate control systems, especially when working on edge repairs or sections with irregular surface texture.

The most expensive mistakes are usually not about the truck price itself. They happen after delivery. Many importers compare only the FOB or CIF number and ignore the cost of spare parts, operator training, and the local availability of chassis service. A chip sealer truck with a lower purchase price can become costly if the pump, burner, or hydraulic components are difficult to replace in the destination country.
