**Q: what does a tanker truck hold**

A tanker truck is designed to carry and transport liquids or gases in bulk. Depending on its configuration and industry use, it may hold fuel such as gasoline or diesel, water, chemicals, milk, liquid food products, or industrial gases. Some tanker trucks are also built for hazardous materials and must meet strict safety and regulatory standards. The exact capacity varies by truck size and tank design, but many tanker trucks can carry anywhere from a few thousand to over 10,000 gallons. If you need a specific type of tanker, the load capacity and material compatibility should be matched to the product being transported.

**Q: what is a lowboy gooseneck trailer**

A lowboy gooseneck trailer is a type of heavy-duty trailer designed to transport oversized and very heavy equipment, such as excavators, bulldozers, and construction machinery. It features a low deck height, which gives it a lower center of gravity and allows for hauling taller loads while staying within road height limits. The “gooseneck” connects to the towing truck at a raised point over the bed, improving stability and weight distribution. This trailer is commonly used in construction, agriculture, and industrial logistics. Depending on size, capacity, and brand, a lowboy gooseneck trailer typically costs around USD 25,000 to USD 90,000.

**Q: how to garbage trucks work**

Garbage trucks work by collecting waste from bins or containers and compacting it inside the vehicle to maximize load capacity. Typically, the truck stops at each pickup point, and the crew or automated arm lifts the container and empties it into a hopper. A hydraulic compaction system then compresses the waste into the main body of the truck. Some models use rear loading, side loading, or front loading depending on the route and collection method. When full, the truck transports the waste to a landfill, transfer station, or recycling facility, where the load is discharged. These trucks are built for heavy-duty, frequent stop-and-go operation.
