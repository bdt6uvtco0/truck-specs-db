## FAQ

**Q: how to transport cargo**

A special truck operates on a standard truck chassis with a power take-off (PTO) driving the specialized equipment: hydraulics raise, lower or drive the working body while the operator controls the cycle from the cab or a panel. Daily checks cover PTO engagement, hydraulic oil level and body mounting bolts. Export units usually run a 260 hp diesel engine matched to the body's demand, keeping fuel burn predictable at roughly 18-25 L/100 km depending on load.

**Q: is asphalt stronger than concrete**

Yes — provided you match the specification to the job and the supplier to reality. A special truck built on a Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a factory warranty is a proven workhorse across Africa, the Middle East and Southeast Asia. The checklist that matters: correct capacity tier, emission standard accepted by your country, pre-shipment inspection, and a spare-parts package in the container. Priced at $28,000-85,000 FOB, most buyers recover the investment within 18-30 months of operation.

**Q: what is rollback tow truck**

A rollback tow truck is a purpose-built towing and recovery vehicle mounted on a truck chassis, engineered for daily commercial duty rather than general haulage. Chinese export versions typically use Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a specialized body, and are supplied with FOB pricing, CE or destination-market certificates and 12-month warranties. Most units ship by RO-RO or flat-rack container with lead times of 30-60 days.
