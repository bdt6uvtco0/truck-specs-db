For overseas importers, the smartest way to choose is to request a full commercial breakdown: chassis brand, boom material thickness, hydraulic system, control brand, spare parts list, and warranty terms. A practical supplier will also provide photos or videos of boom extension, basket rotation, emergency lowering, and stabilization tests. That is how to choose the right truck with fewer surprises.

Useful comparison points include:
1. Working height versus horizontal outreach.
2. Basket load versus actual tool weight and operator count.

4. Stabilizer type and setup speed on uneven ground.
