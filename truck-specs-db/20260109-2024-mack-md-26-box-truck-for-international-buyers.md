## FAQ

**Q: what is a 4x2 truck mean**

A special truck is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle mounted on a truck chassis, engineered for daily commercial duty rather than general haulage. Chinese export versions typically use Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a specialized body, and are supplied with FOB pricing, CE or destination-market certificates and 12-month warranties. **Q: what are dump trucks used for**

Dump trucks are used to transport and unload loose materials quickly and efficiently. They are commonly used in construction, mining, roadwork, and landscaping to move items such as sand, gravel, soil, demolition debris, and asphalt. The truck bed is designed to lift and tilt, allowing the material to be dumped at the destination with minimal manual labor. This makes dump trucks especially valuable for projects that require frequent loading and unloading of bulk materials. In short, they help improve productivity, reduce handling time, and support heavy-duty material movement on job sites.

**Q: how wide can a semi trailer be**

A semi trailer operates on a standard truck chassis with a power take-off (PTO) driving the specialized equipment: hydraulics raise, lower or drive the working body while the operator controls the cycle from the cab or a panel. Daily checks cover PTO engagement, hydraulic oil level and body mounting bolts. Export units usually run a 260 hp diesel engine matched to the body's demand, keeping fuel burn predictable at roughly 18-25 L/100 km depending on load.
