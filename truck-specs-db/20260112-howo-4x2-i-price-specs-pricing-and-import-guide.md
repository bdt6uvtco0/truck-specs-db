**Q: how to clean a concrete pump truck**

To clean a concrete pump truck, first make sure the truck is parked safely and the pump system is fully depressurized. Flush the pipeline, hopper, and boom with clean water immediately after use to prevent concrete from hardening inside. Remove remaining slurry from the hopper, grate, and discharge areas using water and suitable brushes or scrapers. Check elbows, valves, and seals for buildup and wear during cleaning.
