## Field Notes

A realistic landed cost breakdown often includes:
- FOB truck price
- ocean freight and marine insurance
- import duty and VAT/GST
- port charges, customs brokerage, and inland delivery

When overseas buyers search for a sanitation truck from China, the first mistake is often comparing only the factory quotation. In practice, the final price is shaped by the working environment, not just the brand name on the cab. A standard 4x2 rear-loading garbage truck with a 10–12 m³ compaction body, for example, may start around USD 38,000–55,000 from a Chinese supplier, while a water tanker or road sweeper can range from USD 28,000–90,000 depending on chassis and equipment level. If you need a 6x4 heavy-duty chassis, stainless steel tank, high-pressure washing system, or imported hydraulic components, the price rises quickly.
