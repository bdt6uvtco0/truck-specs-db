**Q: what is waste classification**

Waste classification is the process of identifying and grouping waste materials based on their type, source, and handling requirements. It helps determine the correct collection, transport, treatment, recycling, or disposal method. Common categories include municipal solid waste, industrial waste, hazardous waste, construction debris, recyclable materials, and organic waste. In the context of special trucks, waste classification is important because different waste types require different vehicle configurations, such as sealed bodies for hazardous materials, compaction systems for municipal waste, or containers for recyclables. Proper classification improves safety, regulatory compliance, operational efficiency, and environmental performance across waste management operations.
