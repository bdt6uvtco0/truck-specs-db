Field experience shows three common mistakes. First, buyers overspec the reefer but under-spec the chassis, creating weak performance and poor drivability. Second, they ignore axle load matching and end up with overloaded rear suspension. Third, they choose a manufacturer brand with weak after-sales documentation, which makes customs clearance, registration, and maintenance slower.

The chassis determines payload efficiency, fuel use, suspension comfort, braking performance, and parts availability.
