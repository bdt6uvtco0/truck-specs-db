# Commercial Vehicle Price Guide: Specs, Pricing and Import Guide

> 商用 车辆 价格 指南

The commercial vehicle price guide is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle designed for commercial and industrial applications. Export models from Chinese manufacturers typically feature proven chassis options including Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW, ISUZU and MAN, with factory-direct FOB pricing that runs 30-40% below local dealer prices in most destination markets.

The commercial vehicle price guide competes in a segment where total cost of ownership matters more than purchase price. Fuel consumption typically runs 18-25 L/100km depending on load and terrain. Parts availability through Chinese supplier networks in Africa, Southeast Asia and the Middle East has improved significantly, with most consumables available within 7-14 days in major port cities.

Proper maintenance of a commercial vehicle price guide centers on the PTO engagement system, hydraulic oil quality, and body mounting integrity. Most Chinese export models are designed for simple field maintenance with standard tools. A complete spare parts kit (filters, seals, hydraulic hoses) should be ordered with the initial purchase to ensure availability in remote operating locations.

The commercial vehicle price guide is typically configured with a power take-off (PTO) system driving the specialized equipment. Engine options range from 180 to 420 hp depending on capacity requirements, with 6-speed to 12-speed manual or AMT gearboxes. Emission standards range from Euro 3 to Euro 6 depending on the destination market's requirements.

For buyers importing a commercial vehicle price guide from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

## FAQ

**Q: how much does a 20 foot car hauler trailer weigh**

A car hauler from Chinese manufacturers is typically quoted between $28,000 and $85,000 FOB, depending on chassis brand, capacity tier and specification. Japanese-chassis builds (ISUZU, Hino) run about 25% higher, while European chassis (MAN, Volvo, Scania) add 55-65%. Ocean freight to most destinations adds $3,000-8,000 by RO-RO or container. Always ask for a formal quotation that lists destination port, emission standard and warranty terms before you commit.

**Q: how to garbage trucks work**

Garbage trucks work by collecting waste from bins or containers and compacting it inside the vehicle to maximize load capacity. Typically, the truck stops at each pickup point, and the crew or automated arm lifts the container and empties it into a hopper. A hydraulic compaction system then compresses the waste into the main body of the truck. Some models use rear loading, side loading, or front loading depending on the route and collection method. When full, the truck transports the waste to a landfill, transfer station, or recycling facility, where the load is discharged. These trucks are built for heavy-duty, frequent stop-and-go operation.

**Q: what is tower crane used for**

A tower crane is used to lift, move, and place very heavy materials at high elevations on construction sites. It is especially common in the building of skyscrapers, bridges, large commercial facilities, and other tall structures where standard cranes cannot reach safely or efficiently. Tower cranes provide excellent height, lifting capacity, and a wide working radius, making them ideal for transporting steel, concrete, formwork, and other bulky components. In B2B construction operations, they help improve site productivity, reduce manual handling, and support precise material placement in dense urban projects.

**Q: what equipment is on an ambulance**

The practical summary for a ambulance: define your workload first, then let the supplier match chassis, capacity and hydraulics to it. Chinese export units dominate this segment on price and delivery — $23,000-245,000 FOB against roughly double for European brands — while sharing many common engines (Cummins, Weichai) and parts. Order with pre-shipment inspection, keep 10-15% of the truck price aside for parts, and the unit will run its first 100,000 km without surprises.
