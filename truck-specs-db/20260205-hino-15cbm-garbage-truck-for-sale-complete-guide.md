The best purchasing process is practical and document-driven, not casual. First, define the route, waste volume, road conditions, and loading method. Rear loaders work well for mixed household waste and manual collection, while some cities may need a larger bin-lift system or stronger suspension. Second, request a full quotation that includes chassis, body size, compression ratio, engine power, emission standard, and optional equipment such as cameras, alarm systems, and automatic tailgate locking.
