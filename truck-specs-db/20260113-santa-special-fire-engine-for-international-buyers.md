## Field Notes

For comparison, an entry-level small fire engine on a domestic Chinese chassis may cost around USD 35,000 to 70,000. A medium pumper or foam fire engine can range from USD 75,000 to 160,000. Heavy-duty rescue or aerial units may start around USD 180,000 and can exceed USD 500,000 depending on the lift height and imported components. In many cases, the best value comes from pairing a trusted chassis brand with a manufacturer that has proven export experience.

For overseas buyers importing a fire engine from China, the chassis is the foundation of the entire vehicle. In fire and emergency work, the chassis affects reliability, service life, payload, road performance, and the ease of future maintenance. A well-built superstructure on a weak chassis will still create problems in daily use. That is why buyers should compare chassis brands before they compare water tank size or pump power.
