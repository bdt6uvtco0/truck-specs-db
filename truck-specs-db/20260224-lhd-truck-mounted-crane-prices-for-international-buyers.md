## Field Notes

For overseas procurement, technical parameters should be checked in detail before purchase. A standard truck mounted crane configuration includes chassis gross vehicle weight, crane rated lifting moment, boom structure, hydraulic system, and stabilizer legs. Buyers should also confirm whether the crane is mounted behind the cab or at the rear, because this affects load distribution and working radius.

Medium-duty models are widely used in infrastructure and logistics projects.
